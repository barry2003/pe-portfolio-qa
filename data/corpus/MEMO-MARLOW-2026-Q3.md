## Summary
Marlow Packaging remains on plan for the year. The largest customer represents 27.0% of revenue. Headcount is 178.

## Automation and process opportunities
Management estimates 2.5 full-time equivalents are consumed by production scheduling and order entry, a manual process that is a candidate for AI-assisted automation.

## Restatement
Following the Q3 inventory count, Marlow recorded a $0.6M inventory write-down. As a result, TTM EBITDA is restated from $7.4M (September KPI pack) to $6.8M, and gross margin from 22.4% to 21.5%. Revenue is unchanged. This memo supersedes the September KPI pack for these two figures.

## Risks
Principal risks are customer concentration, input cost inflation, and covenant headroom under the senior facility.
