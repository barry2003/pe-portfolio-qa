# Northgate Synthetic Partners Fund I: Portfolio Reporting Policy
SYNTHETIC DATA: fictional fund, companies and numbers created for a software demo. Not real.
Document ID: POLICY-FUND-REPORTING | Dated: 2026-01-15

## Reporting calendar
Portfolio companies deliver a monthly KPI pack by the 10th calendar day after month end. Board memos are delivered within 15 days after quarter end.

## Definitions
Adjusted EBITDA excludes one-time items above $0.25M that are approved by the fund's finance team. Net debt means borrowings plus capital leases less unrestricted cash. Leverage means net debt divided by trailing twelve month EBITDA.

## Restatements and materiality
Any restatement of a reported headline metric by more than 5% of the originally reported figure is material. Material restatements must be notified to the Investment Committee within 5 business days. When two documents conflict, the later dated document governs and the conflict must be disclosed in any summary.
